Approach

Three things that define how I work.

I have negotiated and assessed contracts from the perspective of a supplier, a manufacturer and a retail chain. I know which provisions really matter to each party — and where it is worth holding the line.

Negotiations, correspondence and documentation in Polish, English and German. A contract does not lose its meaning where legal systems and business cultures meet.

I point out the risks that matter commercially and propose solutions that can actually be achieved at the negotiating table — rather than a list of reservations without a recommendation.

Professional qualification

I am a radca prawny — a Polish licensed legal counsel. In Poland this is a regulated profession of public trust, governed by the Act of 6 July 1982 on Legal Counsels (ustawa o radcach prawnych) and the Code of Ethics of Legal Counsels (Kodeks Etyki Radcy Prawnego). I am entered on the list of legal counsels and in active practice.

A radca prawny is authorised to give legal advice and opinions, prepare contracts and other legal documents, and represent clients before courts and authorities in Poland. Professional secrecy applies. The title was conferred in Poland; it is a Polish professional title and not the German title “Rechtsanwalt”.
